About Me

I am a PhD researcher working in the field of aquatic and coastal remote sensing.

My research aims to improve our understanding of carbon dynamics in coastal and marine environments through satellite observations, field measurements, statistical modelling, and machine learning.

I am particularly interested in developing remote-sensing approaches for monitoring particulate organic carbon (POC) and dissolved organic carbon (DOC), and applying these approaches to investigate long-term changes in global coastal carbon systems.

Research

My research integrates satellite observations, in-situ measurements and data-driven modelling to study carbon dynamics in coastal waters.

01

Coastal Carbon Remote Sensing

Developing satellite-based approaches for retrieving and monitoring particulate and dissolved organic carbon in optically complex coastal waters.

02

Ocean Colour Algorithms

Developing adaptive and machine-learning algorithms using ocean-colour observations to improve the retrieval of biogeochemical parameters.

03

Global Coastal Change

Investigating spatial patterns, long-term trends and environmental drivers of coastal carbon using multi-source satellite datasets.
