About

I am a researcher working in aquatic and coastal remote sensing. My work combines satellite observations, field measurements, statistical modelling, and machine learning to study biogeochemical processes in coastal waters.

Research Interests

  • Coastal and ocean remote sensing
  • Particulate organic carbon (POC) and dissolved organic carbon (DOC)
  • Ocean colour remote sensing
  • Global coastal carbon dynamics
  • Machine learning and geospatial data analysis
